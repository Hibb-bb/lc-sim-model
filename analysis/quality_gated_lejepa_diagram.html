Quality-Gated LeJEPA

One star, two telescopes of unequal quality. The embedding is split into a shared slice z_s that is aligned across surveys and a private slice z_p that is not, and the cross-survey pull only ever runs from the worse observation toward the better one.

Which loss touches what

TermSlicePairsGradient reachesWhat it does at the fixed point
L_aug all of z two augmentations of one light curve that observation's branch invariance to crop, subsampling and noise at or below that curve's own error level
L_cross z_s only same star, different telescopes, weighted by σ((q_A − q_B)/τ) the lower-quality branch (higher-quality side is stop-gradiented) z_s(x_B) → E[z_s(x_A) | x_B]; z_s(x_A) keeps whatever A resolves
SIGReg all of z teacher-side embeddings (or quality-weighted pool) every branch it is applied to isotropic Gaussian marginal, no collapse, without needing EMA or negatives

With several telescopes per star, every pair is gated the same way, so the best observation of a star is never the target of a worse one and the shared slice does not shrink to the intersection of all surveys.

If a projector head sits between the encoder and the losses, split after the projector or use one projector per slice; a projector that mixes z_s and z_p before L_cross undoes the separation.
